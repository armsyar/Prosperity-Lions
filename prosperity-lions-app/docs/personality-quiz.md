# Prosperity Lions Personality Quiz (v0.1 Draft)

**Working title:** "Which Prosperity Lion Are You?"
**Length:** 8 scored questions, about 2 minutes, one tap each.
**Reading level:** about age 8+. Short sentences, illustrated scenes.

---

## 1. Design Principles

- **Local flavour, universal feeling.** Every scene is set in a Singapore CNY moment (reunion dinner, yusheng toss, house visiting, Chinatown, snack tins). Each one is really about a universal situation: a family gathering, receiving a gift, a festive crowd, a performance.
- **No wrong answers.** All five options in a question should feel equally fun. Avoid options that sound clearly "better" or "worse".
- **Anyone can play.** People who don't celebrate CNY, or who are visiting for the first time, should feel welcome. Scenes say "family or friends" and "host", and tap-to-explain glossary chips explain terms (hongbao, yusheng, house visiting).
- **Culturally safe.** No pork, alcohol or religious assumptions. No option is tied to a race, age or gender.
- **Light local voice.** Simple English with occasional Mandarin words and light Singlish, never heavy.

---

## 2. Opening Screen

> **New year, new lion!**
> Answer 8 quick questions about your Chinese New Year style, and meet the baby lion who matches you.
> *Been celebrating all your life, or first time? Everyone's welcome!*

**Optional questions (not scored, used to personalise recommendations):**
- Who are you visiting with? *Solo / Couple / Friends / Family with kids / Family with seniors*
- What's your vibe today? *Thrills / Food / Chill / Culture and learning / A bit of everything*

These two questions stay short and never ask for names or contact details.

## 4. Scoring and Tie-Breaks

- Add up points per lion. The highest total is the result. The second-highest is the **runner-up** ("Your second lion is…").
- **Exact tie:** show a final picture-based question with the tied lions ("Which lion would you rather high-five?"). The pick wins the tie.
- Every lion appears once as the main answer in every question (8 questions × 2 points, so a maximum of 16 main points). Secondary points are spread evenly across all five lions.
- **Balance target:** after piloting, each lion should be the result for about 15-25% of players. Rewrite options for any lion that is too popular or too rare.

## 5. Result Screens

Each result: lion illustration, title, short first-person blurb, "You're X% [Lion]", runner-up, and a call to action to say hi.

**Hong Hong (Bold Lion, Red)**
> "You're a natural leader who charges in and gets the party started! Bold, brave and full of energy. I'll be your buddy!"

**Rui Rui (Blessing Lion, Yellow)**
> "You bring the laughter and the luck! Cheeky, friendly, always up to something fun. Hehe, we're going to have a great year!"

**Xing Xing (Growth Lion, Green)**
> "You're an explorer who wants to try everything! Curious, adventurous and just a little bit clumsy. Ooh, let's find something new!"

**Xi Xi (Joy Lion, Pink)**
> "You're the warm heart of every gathering. Gentle and caring, you make everyone feel loved. Come here, I've got a hug for you!"

**Zhi Zhi (Wisdom Lion, Purple)**
> "You're thoughtful, observant and wiser than you let on. Um… I think we're going to get along well."

**After the result:** the lion says hello in its own voice, then offers the quick-reply buttons: *What's on today? · Tell my fortune · Where should I eat? · Teach me a CNY tradition · Just chat.*

## 7. Localisation and Accessibility Checklist

- English first; Mandarin version (simplified) for phase 2 **[TBC]**. Consider Malay and Tamil versions if guest data shows demand.
- Have your cultural advisor or brand team review every scene for sensitivity, including the Mandarin and Hokkien terms (e.g. "huat ah") and lion dance details.
- Halal-friendly and vegetarian-friendly: no scene depends on any specific dish.
- Large tap targets, one-handed use, readable on a bright outdoor screen, screen-reader labels.
- Under 2 minutes; progress bar; can restart any time.

---

## 8. Pilot Plan

1. Test with 50-100 people across ages and backgrounds: Singaporeans of all races, foreign residents, tourists and kids.
2. Check: result distribution, completion rate, drop-off per question, and "Does the result feel like me?" (thumbs up/down).
3. Adjust wording and weights, re-test, then lock before launch.
